What I Believe

Four principles shaped by years of shipping AI in high-stakes enterprise environments.

The data closest to the customer is the most persuasive roadmap input.
In AI products, the 80% bridge matters more than 100% coverage. The art is identifying which 80% delivers disproportionate value.
Influence comes from insight, not authority. The best product decisions start with aggregate data that makes the problem undeniable.
Complexity is a choice. Most enterprise AI products fail not because the models are wrong, but because the integration surface area is unmanaged.
