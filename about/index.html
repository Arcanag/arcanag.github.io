What I Believe

The data closest to the customer is the most persuasive roadmap input. I've seen more roadmaps change from a well-presented conversation analytics dashboard than from any stakeholder request.

In AI products, the 80% bridge matters more than 100% coverage. The art is identifying which 80% delivers disproportionate value and shipping it fast enough to learn.

Influence comes from insight, not authority. The best product decisions I've driven started with aggregate data that made the problem undeniable — not with a title on an org chart.

Complexity is a choice. Most enterprise AI products fail not because the models are wrong, but because the integration surface area is unmanaged.
