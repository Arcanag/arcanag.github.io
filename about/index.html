What I Believe

Four principles shaped by years of shipping AI in high-stakes enterprise environments.

The metric that defines ROI for the customer is the same metric that should anchor the product roadmap.
Product building starts with defining the outcome for the business, then finding the opportunity or problem that unlocks it. The right framework is Outcome → Opportunity → Solution — not the other way around.
Influence comes from insight, not authority. The best product decisions start with aggregate data that makes the problem undeniable.
Complexity is a symptom, not a strategy. Enterprise AI products rarely fail because the model is wrong — they fail because no one owned the seams between systems.
