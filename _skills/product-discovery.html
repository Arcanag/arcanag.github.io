02 / Finding the pain

The real problem was at login.

I looked at the whole call journey, not just the model. The biggest leak was a DTMF timing bug at the OTP step.

What everyone assumed

“The AI model isn’t accurate enough.”

Intent accuracy was 70%, so the model took the blame.

What the data showed

Failed at OTP47%
Dead end in the flow23%

Each rate measured on its own base

Callers describe problems, not requests.

A more accurate model still couldn’t tell similar problems apart — so I proposed grouping intents under root problems.

03 / Again, on a second product

Not the speech engine. The intents.

On a global smartphone brand’s inbound voicebot, I reviewed 698 detractor responses, 500 agent transfers and the raw transcripts before agreeing what to fix.

The roadmap moved from “fix the speech engine” to intent fixes, a fast metric clean-up for silent callers, and a flow change scoped as an A/B test rather than a blind rewrite.
