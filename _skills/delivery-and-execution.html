01 / The situation

30 use cases. Each from scratch.

For a Tier-1 private bank, every use case needed its own APIs, NLU changes, flows and multi-round testing — through a 27-step lifecycle, on an air-gapped system that was already live.

03 / The gates

Seven gates before day one.

Each use case waited on seven bank-side prerequisites before our pipeline could start. I tracked them as dependencies, not as excuses — and chased them four levels deep in the bank.

  1. 1Requirement documents
  2. 2API documentation
  3. 3Parameter definitions
  4. 4Routing details
  5. 5SMS content
  6. 6Production recordings
  7. 7Test data
Every deploy A formal plan of action, an email approval chain, and production and disaster-recovery sites updated together.
