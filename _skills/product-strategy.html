01 / The situation

Far behind its contract.

I inherited a bank voicebot with 30 use cases still to deliver on an air-gapped system — and new regulatory work arriving mid-stream.

The question wasn’t what to build. It was what to build first.

02 / The bet

Trust first. Scale second.

Shipping 30 use cases onto a bot nobody could measure would only multiply the problem. The strategy was to earn trust in the numbers, then scale.

The bet

Measurement before features

Rebuild how calls are counted, so every later decision has a number the bank accepts.

What it unlocks

Fixes aimed at the real leak

Trusted data pointed at login, not the model — the fix that moved contained calls.

What it pays for

Scale the bank believes

30 use cases shipped in 5 waves, each measured the same way.
