02 / The safety net

From incidents to a runbook.

The 7-minute outage was a process gap: no mandatory client approval for maintenance windows. So the fixes were process, not heroics.

03 / The gate

Caught at review. Fixed the same day.

On the next portfolio, compliance moved upstream: a requirements-gap gate blocks development until every exposure is resolved.

Exposure 1

Offer disclosed to third parties

A script set would have revealed a customer’s offer to third-party contacts — forbidden by the client’s own requirements.

Exposure 2

Promotion labelled as service

A promotional flow was labelled as a service call — the distinction telecom regulation turns on.

Standing gate

No build until resolved

Both fixed before development started; the requirements-gap check is now a standing gate on every new build.
