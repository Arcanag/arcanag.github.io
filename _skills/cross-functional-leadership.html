01 / The situation

One team. Three jobs.

Maintenance, new delivery and regulatory work all at once, across engineering, conversation design, QA and the bank. Things broke.

02 / The structure

Organised around outcomes.

I organised the work into three squads so each group owned a clear result — and the bank knew exactly who to call for what.

03 / The safety net

From incidents to shared rules.

Squads only work if hand-offs are agreed. With engineering and the bank, I put in place client-approval gates and on-call rotations.
