01 / The situation

Everyone agreed. Nobody agreed on what first.

A bank’s voicebot handled 100K+ calls a day but was far behind its contract.

800K
contained calls/mo promised
20K
contained calls/mo actual
70%
intent accuracy (SLU)

02 / The options

Three options. One call.

Each was reasonable. They differed in how fast they’d help and whether the fix would last.

FAST + LASTING A · Retrain B · Flows C · Hybrid HOW LONG THE FIX LASTS → TIME UNTIL IT HELPS →

A

Retrain the AI model

6+ months · high risk. Thorough, but slow.

B

Redesign the call flows

2–3 months · low risk. Quick wins, no lasting fix.

C · Chosen

Hybrid

Fix the biggest leak now, retrain in parallel, give security its own squad.

Chart positions are a judgement, not measurements

Retraining first would have fixed the wrong thing.

The pressure was to improve the AI model. Once I rebuilt how calls were measured, the data pointed somewhere else.

03 / What decided it

The data said: look at login.

Failed at OTP47%
Hit a dead end in the flow23%

Each rate measured on its own base

04 / The roadmap

Order mattered more than speed.

Each step had to make the next one possible.

First

Fix how calls are measured

Why — we couldn’t steer by numbers nobody trusted.

Then

Fix OTP, retrain intent recognition

Why — 47% of callers were failing at OTP.

Scale

Ship 30 use cases in 5 waves

Why — that’s where the 800K contained calls would come from.

In parallelA dedicated squad absorbed RBI security mandates, so they didn’t stall the roadmap.

Still short of the target. Next time, I’d fix measurement in week one.
