01 / The situation

Resolution sets the price.

On a second major private bank’s collections voicebot, billing depends on a band set by value resolution. A few points of resolution can outweigh thousands of extra paid leads.

One book collected more in a month and still earned less, because it slipped a band. That is when resolution stopped being treated as the client’s KPI and started being treated as ours.

03 / The business case

Capacity is a P&L question.

Growing from ~1.2M to a 4.5M calls-a-day target needed about 1,000 more concurrent channels. I built the business case — the investment against modest visible revenue, justified by dial-attempt capacity and lower NPS risk.
