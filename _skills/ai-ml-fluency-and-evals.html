01 / The situation

Wins claimed. Quality unmeasured.

A leading private-sector bank had moved a lending bot from NLP to an LLM. Everyone called it a win — nobody had measured it, or what it cost in quality.

~1.6M
calls / 30 days on the lending flow
0.62%
promise-to-pay per dialled lead on NLP
2
LLM bots live, quality unmeasured

A win reported without its quality debt isn’t a measurement. It’s marketing.

02 / The cutover

4.2× lift. Measured honestly.

Same engagement conditions, a decomposed funnel, and numbers checked against the orchestration database rather than the dashboard. A clean before/after — not an A/B test, and labelled that way.

03 / The audit

74 of 100 calls. Defective.

So we listened to the calls behind the number. The 4.2× was real. So was the debt — and both shipped together, as a prioritised fix list.

In hindsight

Instrument before migrating. We measured the win after the cutover — baseline first would have saved weeks.
