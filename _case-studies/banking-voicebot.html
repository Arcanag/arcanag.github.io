05 / The broken metric

A number nobody trusted.

Containment had plunged, and nobody was confident the calculation was even right.

Root cause 1

Compromised analytics

50+ bugs in tracking code. Max-retry hang-ups counted as contained. Missing data for 10+ intents.

Root cause 2

Metric misalignment

No shared definition of success. Containment confused with resolution. Team and client measuring differently.

Root cause 3

Rushed product design

No graceful fallbacks. Three-attempt hang-ups counted as contained. Incomplete flows.

I halted optimisation and built the analytics funnel from scratch.

08 / The decision

Three options. One call.

Balance quick wins now against a fix that lasts.

Option A

Full SLU retrain

6+ months · high risk in an air-gapped stack. Comprehensive but delayed.

Option B

Flow redesign only

2–3 months · low risk. Quick wins, no lasting fix.

Option C · Chosen

Hybrid

Immediate + ongoing · medium risk. Auth and flow fixes now, SLU expansion in parallel, security absorbed alongside.

10 / What went wrong

Not every bet paid off.

Case studies that only show wins aren’t useful.

  1. 01

    Architectural mismatch

    Intent accuracy rose from 70% to 91%, but the model was solution-oriented while customers describe problems. Complex utterances triggered conflicting intents.

  2. 02

    System instability

    15 incidents in 3 months — flaws in the production-to-disaster-recovery (DR) switchover, plus bank API failures and network drops.

  3. 03

    A maintenance-window failure

    7 minutes of unplanned downtime during live calls, because no mandatory client approval existed for maintenance windows. A process gap, not a technical one.

  4. 04

    Security scope creep

    RBI-mandated compliance consumed up to 90% of infrastructure bandwidth in peak weeks.

One caller, three intents

“I am at an airport lounge and trying to make a payment but the transaction is not working.”

12 / In hindsight

Five things I’d do differently.

  1. 01

    Fix measurement first

    We chased 8 lakh contained calls against a system we couldn’t trust. Trusted analytics should have been week one.

  2. 02

    Enterprise AI governance is product management

    Plans of action, approval chains, closed-user-group testing and maintenance windows are the discipline that separates enterprise AI from a demo.

  3. 03

    Single points of failure are PM risks

    One engineer for prod-to-DR switchovers and undocumented recovery steps. Build operational muscle before you need it.

  4. 04

    Influence through data breaks deadlocks

    Everyone blamed the NLU model. The funnel showed 47% dropping at OTP, and that aligned everyone on a hybrid fix.

  5. 05

    Absorb scope without losing the thread

    A dedicated security squad shielded core teams from context switching when RBI mandates arrived mid-sprint.

In cross-functional deadlocks, aggregate data is the only objective tiebreaker.
